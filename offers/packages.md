## First Client Strategy

Use the "Portfolio Builder" offer to get your first 3-5 clients:
- Price: €400 (normally €1,400)
- Full Business package at discount
- In exchange: testimonial + portfolio permission
- Only for first 3-5 clients, then raise to full price

**Script:**
"I'm building my portfolio and looking for 5 local businesses to work with at a significant discount. In exchange, I just ask for a testimonial and the ability to showcase your site in my portfolio."

## Closing Script

**On a call:**
"Based on what you've told me, I think our Business package would be perfect. It's €1,400, includes 5 pages, mobile design, SEO, and we can have it live in 2 weeks. If we start this week, your new site is live by [date]. Want to move forward?"

**Handling "too expensive":**
"I understand. We could start with just a landing page for €700 and build from there. Would that work better?"
