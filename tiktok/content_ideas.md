# TikTok Content Strategy for Web Design Agency

Complete guide to creating viral content that attracts small business owners.

---

## 📊 Content Pillars (Rotate Between These)

### 1. Website Roasts (30% of content)
Reviewing bad websites, explaining what's wrong, showing impact.

### 2. Before/After Transformations (25% of content)
Showing redesign results, client wins, visual transformations.

### 3. Business Owner Education (25% of content)
Teaching why websites matter, common mistakes, quick tips.

### 4. Behind the Scenes (10% of content)
Your process, day in the life, client calls (with permission).

### 5. Storytelling/Results (10% of content)
Client success stories, case studies, revenue impact.

## 📱 POSTING STRATEGY

### Optimal Times (Test for your audience)
- **Best**: 7-9 AM, 12-1 PM, 7-10 PM (local time)
- **Weekdays** for business owners
- **Weekends** work too—they're often planning ahead

### Posting Frequency
- **Minimum**: 1x per day
- **Ideal**: 2-3x per day
- **Consistency > Volume**: Better to do 1/day forever than 5/day for a week

### Content Mix (Per Week)
- 2-3 Website Roasts
- 2 Educational Videos
- 1-2 Before/After
- 1 Behind the Scenes
- 1 Story/Case Study

## 🔧 PRODUCTION TIPS

### Equipment Needed
- Phone with decent camera (any modern smartphone)
- Ring light or window light
- Tripod or phone stand
- External mic (optional but helps)
- Screen recording software

### Screen Recording Setup
1. Record your screen showing bad websites
2. Voice over with your commentary
3. Use zoom-ins to highlight issues
4. Add text callouts for key points

### Editing Style
- Quick cuts (don't let any clip drag)
- Text on screen for key points
- Zoom effects on problem areas
- Trending sounds (optional—talking can work better)
- Captions always (most watch without sound)

## 📈 GROWTH TACTICS

### Engage Before Posting
- Comment on local business content 15 min before posting
- Reply to every comment within the first hour
- Ask questions to boost engagement

### Hashtag Strategy
- Mix of sizes: #smallbusiness (huge), #websitedesigner (medium), #[city]business (small)
- 3-5 hashtags max
- Put in caption or first comment

### Cross-Promotion
- Turn TikToks into Instagram Reels
- Post to YouTube Shorts
- Share in local Facebook groups (carefully)
- LinkedIn for B2B angle

### Collaboration Ideas
- Duet with local business TikTokers
- Review/feature local businesses
- Partner with business coaches
- Guest on local business podcasts

## ⚡ QUICK WIN: Your First 10 TikToks

1. **Intro video**: "I help small businesses get more customers with better websites"
2. **Local restaurant roast**: Find a bad one, review it respectfully
3. **Educational**: "3 signs your website is losing customers"
4. **Before/after**: Even if it's a mockup or personal project
5. **Local barbershop roast**: Different niche, same format
6. **Tip**: "Your phone number needs to be clickable"
7. **Reaction**: React to a business with a bad website
8. **Educational**: "Why mobile matters for [niche]"
9. **Story**: Even if it's hypothetical at first
10. **CTA video**: "I do free website audits—here's what I look for"

Start with these 10. Refine your style. Then scale up.

---

## 🚫 WHAT NOT TO DO

- Don't be mean-spirited (roast the site, not the person)
- Don't use copyrighted music for talking videos
- Don't make content too long (15-45 seconds is ideal starting out)
- Don't ignore comments
- Don't post without a hook
- Don't oversell in every video
- Don't copy other creators exactly
- Don't give up after 10 videos

---

Good luck! Consistency beats perfection. Start posting. 🚀
